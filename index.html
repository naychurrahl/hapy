Our Story & Values

Company Profile

Hypertension Awareness and Prevention Initiative (HYPA) Nigeria is a non-governmental organization registered in 2025, dedicated to combating hypertension, a major public health challenge affecting over 30% of Nigerian adults. As a new entity, HYPA Nigeria is poised to contribute to President Bola Tinubu’s Renewed Hope Agenda by promoting healthy lifestyles and living, reducing the burden of hypertension through awareness, prevention, and community empowerment.

Our initiatives focus on early detection, education, and strengthening primary healthcare systems to address the low awareness (29%), treatment (12%), and control (2.8%) rates reported in Nigeria.

Our Vision

A world where every individual has access to quality education, healthcare, and economic opportunities, enabling them to reach their full potential and contribute to their communities.

Our Motto

"Healthy Living, Stronger Nigeria"

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities through sustainable programs in education, healthcare, and economic development, creating lasting change through partnership, innovation, and compassion.

Our Objectives

  • To make campaign and awareness in the 36 states in Nigerian including FCT.
  • To campaign for healthy lifestyle.
  • To campaign for the control of high/low blood pressure in communities.
  • To reduce the risk of death caused by high/low blood pressure.
  • To do proper checkups to individuals with high/low blood pressure, and give them proper medication and BP machines for regular monitoring of blood pressure.
  • Partnering with all the state government in Nigeria and FCT to reduce the risk of sudden death in Nigeria caused by blood pressure. Like Stroke, Cardiac arrest, Heart failure, Blindness, Erectile dysfunction.
  • To promote and improve on the present administration agenda on Renew hope agenda by living a healthy life.
